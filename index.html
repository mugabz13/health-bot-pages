Health Bot

Health Bot is a personal-use assistant tool for the account owner. It helps with fitness, nutrition, and weight coaching by reading selected Google Health data the owner chooses to share.

  • Health Bot is not made by Google, is not endorsed by Google, and is not a Google product. Google Health is only a data source the owner may choose to connect.
  • Health Bot does not provide medical advice and does not replace a clinician, diagnosis, or treatment.
  • This site is not a dashboard. It does not show health data, and it does not offer sign-in.

What it does

  • Pulls read-only health and fitness information from Google Health (for example weight and body composition that may arrive from a scale app through Health Connect, plus activity, sleep, and related metrics when available).
  • Uses that information, together with meals and habits the owner shares in chat, to give coaching summaries and reminders.
  • Is intended for one person’s own account, not as a public consumer product or multi-user service.

How Google Health connects

  1. The owner authorizes Health Bot in Google’s sign-in / consent flow.
  2. Health Bot uses that authorization to call Google’s Health APIs with read-only access.
  3. Pulled data is used only to support the owner’s coaching and tracking conversations.

Health Bot does not ask Google for write access to change health records.

Privacy

Contact

Questions about this personal tool can be sent to the developer contact email configured on the Health Bot Google Cloud OAuth branding settings (the owner’s project support email).
