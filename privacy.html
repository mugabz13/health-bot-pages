Health Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 (America/New_York)

This policy describes how the personal-use Health Bot tool handles Google Account and Google Health information for the person who authorized it (the “owner”).

It is written from the known current implementation, not as a full legal warranty. Where details are unknown or changeable, that is called out below.

These public pages are static files. They do not include forms, sign-in, analytics, or scripts, and they do not display health records. Hosting access logs, if any, are controlled by GitHub Pages. This policy does not describe that host’s logs or retention, and it does not mean Google has verified Health Bot.

Who this is for

Health Bot is a personal tool operated with the owner’s own Google Cloud OAuth application (“Health Bot”). It is meant for the owner’s fitness, nutrition, and weight coaching—not for selling access to other people’s health data.

Health Bot is not made by Google, is not endorsed by Google, and is not a Google product. It does not provide medical advice and does not replace a clinician, diagnosis, or treatment.

Google data we request

Through Google’s OAuth consent screen, Health Bot may request read-only access to Google Health–related scopes such as:

  • Activity and fitness
  • Health metrics and measurements
  • Sleep
  • Nutrition
  • Profile (basic Google Health profile fields)

Exact scope names and wording are controlled by Google’s consent screen at authorization time.

In practice, the tool’s Google Health pull can retrieve metric families such as weight, body fat, height, heart-related daily metrics, exercise sessions, sleep summaries, steps, active minutes, distance, calories, floors, and profile fields when present in Google Health. Some authorized scopes (for example nutrition) may be granted even when a given pull does not export every possible data type.

Data in Google Health may originate from apps and devices the owner connects elsewhere (for example a scale app syncing through Health Connect into Google Health). Health Bot reads what Google Health exposes; it does not replace those source apps.

How the data is used

Authorized Google Health data is used to:

  • Summarize weigh-ins and related metrics for the owner
  • Support fitness, nutrition, and weight coaching in chat
  • Power owner-requested reminders or digests when those routines are enabled

It is not used to write or alter Google Health records through this connection.

How the data is processed and stored (known implementation)

Based on the current scripts and how the assistant is used:

Authorization tokens

After the owner consents, OAuth tokens are stored locally on the assistant’s private workspace machine (as a local token file, with restrictive file permissions in the current script). Tokens are refreshed as needed to call Google’s APIs. Token file paths, client IDs, and secrets are not published on this site.

API pulls

The tool calls Google’s Health API for the authorized user (users/me) and may write CSV exports of pulled metrics into a private workspace folder for analysis and coaching.

Assistant processing

Pulled metrics and related chat context are processed by the owner’s assistant (Grok Bot) to produce coaching replies, reminders, and optional journal/memory notes the owner has asked for. That means health-related content can appear in chat history and in assistant memory/journal entries the product stores for the owner.

What we do not claim

This policy does not claim that data is “never shared,” “never leaves the device,” or “always encrypted at rest/in transit” beyond what is stated above. The assistant product may process messages and tool output through its normal AI and storage systems. Exact third-party subprocessors, retention clocks, and encryption details for the assistant product are not fully specified in the Health Bot scripts reviewed for this page.

Sharing

  • Health Bot is not designed as a marketplace or public feed of the owner’s health data.
  • Coaching answers may be shown to the owner in chat. If the owner uses group chats, shared bots, or forwards messages, those channels can expose content the owner chooses to share.
  • Revoking Google access (below) stops future Google API access. It does not by itself erase past chat logs, memory/journal notes, or local CSV exports already created.

Retention and deletion (honest limits)

  • Google-side access: The owner can revoke Health Bot’s Google Account access at any time (see below). After revoke, new pulls should fail until the owner authorizes again.
  • Local exports / chat / memory: There is no automated “wipe all Health Bot copies on revoke” guarantee in the current implementation. The owner can ask the assistant to delete known local export files and to clear or stop journaling weigh-in notes. Product-level chat retention follows the assistant product’s own settings and policies, which are outside this script-level description.

Unresolved items for a fuller policy (see gaps note for the maintainer) include formal retention periods, backup copies, and a documented wipe checklist.

How to revoke Google access

To review or remove apps that have access to your Google Account data:

  1. Open Google’s linked-apps / connections page in your Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/connections
  2. Open Access to your Google Account (wording may vary slightly).
  3. Select Health Bot (or the name shown on the consent screen).
  4. Review permissions, then choose Remove access and confirm.

Official Google help: Manage links between your Google Account & apps from other developers.

Removing access stops the app from using your Google Account data going forward. It does not automatically delete information the tool already stored outside Google (local exports, chat, memory). Ask the assistant or delete those copies separately if you want them removed.

Children

Health Bot is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 13 (or under the digital-consent age in the owner’s region).

Changes

If the owner updates how Health Bot uses Google data in a material way, this policy text should be updated and the “Last updated” date changed.

Contact

Use the developer / support email configured on the Health Bot Google Cloud OAuth branding page for privacy questions about this personal tool. That address is not printed on this site.
